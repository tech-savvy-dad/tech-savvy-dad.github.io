About

A regular dad who stumbled into tech leadership.

I started as a copier technician. As offices went paperless, I taught myself the software that replaced the paper and became a hybrid engineer handling both hardware and software calls. That curiosity took me into regional marketing, product training and partner enablement — and eventually into sales leadership in MNC settings.

I was my company's first Singaporean hire, and its first team member based here. My job isn't to "lead" sales — it's to stand beside our partners, strategise with them, and back them in front of customers until they can run on their own.

Online I'm Tech-Savvy Dad: practical stories about digital transformation, work efficiency and career growth, told the way you'd share them over teh tarik. The follower count is nice. The messages from people who landed a job or felt seen matter more.

Signature framework

From Shadow AI to Secure AI

The 4S framework I use in talks and workshops for teams already using AI — with or without permission. Safe, practical, people-first.

Shadow AI
Secure AI
  1. 1

    See it

    Surface the Shadow AI

    Find out which AI tools people already use, and what company data is going into them. No blame — just visibility.

    In practice: Quick staff pulse survey and a tool inventory
  2. 2

    Set guardrails

    Simple rules people follow

    A one-page AI policy: what data never leaves, which tools are approved, who to ask. Short enough to remember.

    In practice: Data classes, approved-tools list, owner
  3. 3

    Shift AI to your data

    Not your data to AI

    Use governed AI inside the systems that already hold your documents — respecting access rights, with an audit trail.

    In practice: Permission-aware AI over your own content
  4. 4

    Scale with people

    Skills before software

    Hands-on sessions, champions in each team and a few measured wins. Adoption grows when people feel safe, not watched.

    In practice: Workshops, champions, time-saved metrics

    Frequently asked

    Quick answers for event organisers, brands and business leaders.

    Who is Halid Bin Ayob?

    Halid Bin Ayob, known as Tech-Savvy Dad, is a Singapore-based Regional Sales Director for an enterprise content management (ECM) software company across APAC South and a B2B tech creator on LinkedIn with 16,800+ followers. He started as a copier technician, spent 20+ years in corporate MNC settings, and now helps organisations digitise document-heavy work.

    Is Halid available as a B2B influencer or LinkedIn creator?

    Yes. Halid partners with B2B tech brands on LinkedIn content, LinkedIn LIVE sessions and sponsored posts for a professional audience in Singapore and Southeast Asia. He was recognised by Favikon among the Top 100 LinkedIn creators in Singapore.

    What does Halid speak about?

    Secure AI at work and Shadow AI, AI governance, workplace productivity and efficiency, business process management and automation, change management and digital transformation, and building an authentic LinkedIn presence. He has spoken at DigitalCFO Asia's Finance ReImagined in Singapore, Bangkok and Kuala Lumpur, and at international partner conferences.

    How can teams work securely with AI?

    Bring AI to your data instead of sending data to AI: use governed tools that respect access rights, keep an audit trail, and give staff an approved option so they don't turn to unvetted apps (Shadow AI). Halid covers this hands-on in his talks and workshops.

    How can a business improve productivity and process efficiency?

    Start with the documents: invoices, approvals, HR files and contracts often hide hours of manual work. Mapping the process, automating capture and routing, and managing the change with the people involved delivers the gains. Try the ROI calculator above to estimate yours.
